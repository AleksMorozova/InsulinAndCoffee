# Safety wording

Insulin & Coffee is a support and memory tool, not a medical decision maker.

## Good wording

- “Estimated carbs”
- “Based on your saved history”
- “Similar previous meals”
- “This looks uncertain”
- “Please check manually”
- “No matching saved meals found”
- “I can summarize your saved notes, but I cannot determine a medical dose.”

## Avoid wording

- “Correct insulin dose”
- “Guaranteed safe”
- “You should inject”
- “This will prevent hypo/hyper”
- “Medically accurate”

## UI behavior

When confidence is low:

- show a warning
- show sources
- let user edit manually
- avoid automatic save of uncertain values
